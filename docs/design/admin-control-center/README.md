## What these are, stated as the owner stated it

**The 12Circle+ Admin design — Fitonist-DERIVED, MODIFIED for 12Circle+.**

- They are **not** the original unmodified Fitonist product.
- The visual system they show is the **12Circle+** confirmation visual system. **The Fitonist identity
  is not the 12Circle+ identity.**
- The screens carry their own disclaimers — *"All figures are sample design-state data"* and
  *"Sample data"* — so the figures are design-state, not product data.

## What they establish

**The adopted Admin IA, confirmed visually** — six top-level items, matching the owner's `CONF-D1`
decision exactly: **Dashboard · People ▾ · Ecosystem ▾ · Trust ▾ · Operations ▾ · Settings**, with
status dots on Trust and Operations.

**The visual system direction** — dark surface; a violet primary accent with an amber secondary; green
for operational/positive and red for critical; large light-weight numeric readouts; a card-based grid
with generous radii; pill segmented controls for time ranges (Today / 7 days / 30 days / 90 days).

**Product framing observable in the screens** — a staging banner (*"Staging environment — changes here
do not affect members"*), a `Staging` environment pill, a `D. Mac · Platform admin` identity, and the
*Needs your attention* drawer whose footer reads *"Actions open the item. **Nothing is changed from
this screen.**"*

## What they do NOT establish

- **Token values.** A rendering is not a token set: no hex values, type scale, or spacing scale.
  `CONF-D6`'s *"locked identity package"* is **not** delivered by screenshots alone.
- **The domain → navigation mapping.** Every dropdown (`People`, `Ecosystem`, `Trust`, `Operations`) is
  **closed in all four screens**, so no sub-navigation is visible. The nine unplaced Admin domains
  remain unplaced.
- **The 11 Admin states.** Still *"ENUMERATED, NOT DESIGNED … no frames"* — these four screens are
  populated states only.
- **Responsive behaviour, component specifications, iconography.**
- **The approved Admin screen package.** `CONF-D4` — *"a brief cannot serve as design authority"* — is a
  separate gate, and four dashboard renderings are not a design package.
